ABOUT US

Members

Del Rosario, Richiebelle | Managing Director & Head Curator

A former literature professor from University Of Caloocan City, Rich realized that many great Filipino titles were hard to find in mainstream malls. She opened the shop to give indie Philippine publishers and local authors a permanent home. She is the heart of the business, often seen with a cup of Barako coffee in hand, chatting with regulars.

  • Core Skills: Deep knowledge of Filipiniana, networking with local university presses, and archival preservation (keeping books safe from humidity).
  • Inspiration: The resilience of Philippine grassroots movements and the storytelling of Lualhati Bautista and Nick Joaquin.
  • Signature Move: Being able to recommend the perfect "hidden gem" poetry book based on a customer’s current mood or heartbreak.

    Capua, Casey | Creative Lead & Community Manager

    A Fine Arts graduate and active member of the local Zine scene, Cas ensures the shop is more than just a place to buy books—it's a "third space." She designed the shop’s mural and manages the small gallery corner featuring local illustrators and sticker artists.

  • Core Skills: Graphic design, workshop facilitation (calligraphy and zine-making), and TikTok/Reels storytelling.
  • Inspiration: Filipino Komiks culture and the vibrant, "do-it-yourself" energy of Cubao Expo and Maginhawa Street.
  • Signature Move: Organizing "Silent Reading Nights" and Komiket pop-ups that draw a crowd of young Gen Z creatives.
    		•

    Borlagdatan, Nathaniel | Operations & Logistics Manager

    Nat is the one who figured out how to make a physical bookstore survive in the age of e-commerce. With a background in supply chain management, he handles the "diskarte" side of things—managing the shop’s Shopee/Lazada storefronts and ensuring books reach provinces from Batanes to Jolo.

  • Core Skills: Inventory systems, digital marketing, and "last-mile" logistics in the Philippine context.
  • Inspiration: The efficiency of Binondo traders mixed with the aesthetic of modern minimalist cafes in Makati.
  • Signature Move: Finding a way to source a rare, out-of-print textbook from a remote warehouse within 48 hours.

    OUR MILESTONE

    Year 1: The "First Page" (Foundational Roots)
    • Securing the Space: Signed a lease for a dusty corner in a heritage building. We spent more on termite treatment and scrubbing the floors than on actual furniture.
    • Opening Day: We didn't have a big marketing budget, so we just opened the doors and served free Barako coffee. We sold out of our "Filipiniana" section in three days just by word of mouth.
    • The First Regular: A student from a nearby university became our first "true" customer, spending her lunch breaks on our floor because we didn't have enough chairs yet.
    Year 2: Survival and "The Shopee Grind"
    • E-Commerce Integration: Nat launches the official Shopee and Lazada storefronts, implementing a "bulky-proof" packaging system to ensure books survive the tropical humidity and rainy season shipping..
    • Nationwide Shipping: Successfully fulfilling the first bulk orders to readers in Batanes and Tawi-Tawi, proving that distance isn't a barrier to local literature.
    • Social Media Breakout: One of Casey’s "Aesthetic Filipiniana" TikTok tours goes viral, leading to a 300% increase in foot traffic from Gen Z readers.
    Year 3: The "Third Space" (Community Authority)
    • The Workshop Series: Launching "The Maker’s Nook," a monthly event featuring Baybayin workshops, zine-making classes, and poetry slams led by Casey..
    • Institutional Partnerships: Partnering with local universities (like UP, Ateneo, and UST) to become an official distributor of their academic and literary presses.
    • The First "Zine Fest": Hosting an independent zine festival within the shop, supporting over 50 local grassroots artists.
    Year 4: Sustainability & Expansion (The Green Leaf)
    • Mobile Bookshop: Nat develops a "Bookshop on Wheels" (a refurbished jeepney or van) that travels to local weekend markets and schools in neighboring provinces like Rizal or Laguna.
    • Eco-Friendly Shift: Transitioning to 100% plastic-free packaging, using recycled abaca paper and soy-based inks for all shop branding.
    • The "Writer-in-Residence" Program: Rich establishes a small fund to support one local author a year, providing them a quiet space in the shop to finish their manuscript.
    Year 5: Literary Legacy (The Full Circle)
    • In-House Publishing: Every Chapter Counts launches its first small-press imprint, publishing a collection of short stories from Mindanaoan writers.
    • Flagship Expansion: Renovating the original shop to include a full-service café serving local beans (Sultan Kudarat and Sagada) to complement the reading experience.
    • National Recognition: Being named "Independent Bookstore of the Year" by a major Philippine literary body.
